01

A library that keeps up

Browse channels by group, search movies and series, and save your favourites. Resume an unfinished movie from where you left off.

02

Picture and sound your way

Choose the stream’s audio and subtitle tracks. Import an SRT/VTT file or search OpenSubtitles using your own API key.

03

From a small window to full screen

Keep watching in a picture-in-picture window above other windows, or go full screen. Pause, channel switching and picture fit are available in the player controls.

Your first viewing session

Three steps to Aava.

  1. Download and openChoose the package for your operating system and open the app.
  2. Connect your own serviceEnter your service’s Xtream Codes details or M3U URL in the app.
  3. Choose something to watchOpen a channel, movie or series. Without your own source, you can explore Aava’s demo library.
